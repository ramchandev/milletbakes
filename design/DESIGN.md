## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an artisanal, wholesome, and deeply nourishing culinary philosophy centered on ancient grains, mindful baking, and earthy luxury. It bridges traditional handcrafted grain baking with a crisp, modern direct-to-consumer and corporate gifting digital experience. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Rustic Craft** with **Modern Editorial Commerce**. It pulls inspiration from flour-dusted workbenches, warm terracotta bread ovens, unbleached parchment papers, and hand-stamped harvest packaging. The tone is welcoming, honest, grounded, and appetizing—evoking sensory warmth without visual clutter. 

Visual accents feature embossed insignia seals, grain motifs, hand-lettered touchpoints, and soft rounded container framing that preserves tactile bakery intimacy while delivering structured, accessible ordering and enterprise booking flows.

## Colors

The palette draws strictly from natural oven tones, raw whole grains, and botanical field hues:

- **Primary (`#3A1D13`) — Roast Cocoa / Dark Crust:** A dense, rich chocolate brown that anchors headlines, primary action buttons, deep footer zones, and core structural outlines. It replaces stark black with an organic, hearth-fired depth.
- **Secondary (`#C86D46`) — Terracotta Spice:** A toasted cinnamon accent used for interactive highlights, promotional tags, urgency cues, and secondary calls-to-action.
- **Tertiary (`#4E6E58`) — Herbal Sage:** An earthy seed green used for nutritional callouts ("No Maida", "Zero Sugar", "Organic"), wholesome ingredient indicators, and seasonal freshness badges.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FBF7F0`) — Warm Stone / Parchment:** The unbleached grain base applied to body backgrounds, elevated card surfaces, and warm neutral fills, paired with `#F5EFEB` for secondary paneling.

Contrast is maintained rigorously: primary dark brown text on warm parchment yields an accessible contrast ratio exceeding 9:1, ensuring high legibility across all age groups.

## Typography

The type system blends bold, sculptural character with humanistic clarity:

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in **Epilogue**, chosen for its confident geometric curves, heavy weight availability, and vintage artisan bakery presence. In uppercase or title-case, its substantial weight resembles cast-iron bakehouse signage and woodblock stamp impressions.
- **Body & Functional UI:** Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering warm, open terminals and exceptional legibility across ingredient lists, product specs, and pricing grids.
- **Micro-Copy & Stamp Elements:** Uppercase wide-tracked variants (`label-stamp`) are specified for circular trust seals, batch numbers, dietary badges ("100% NATURAL", "SUGAR FREE"), and order tickers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid for desktop (max-width 1280px) and reflows into a single or 2-column stacked flow on handheld screens.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` section margins. Product catalog sections allow alternating asymmetrical hero arrangements alongside regular 3-up product columns.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Product grids convert to 2-up displays.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex hampers and multi-product bundles reflow into horizontally scrollable swipe trays with clear peek-affordance.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Generous inter-section buffers (`space-2xl` / 64px) preserve the unhurried, editorial feel of high-end bakery publications, while component internals stay tight and structured (`space-sm` to `space-md`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical bakery and pantry cues rather than stark digital drops:

1. **Parchment Layering:** Depth is established primarily through tone-on-tone surface stacking—placing `#FFFFFF` cards over `#FBF7F0` canvas, enclosed by subtle warm hairline borders (`#EBDCCF`).
2. **Warm Ambient Shadows:** When floating elements require separation (e.g., sticky order trays, floating product cards, flyout cart drawer), use low-opacity, warm-tinted shadows:
   - *Soft Rest:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(58, 29, 19, 0.06)`
   - *Hover Lift:* `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(58, 29, 19, 0.12)`
3. **Stamped Inset Depth:** For dietary badges, nutritional meters, and voucher codes, apply an inset grain shadow (`inset 0 1px 3px rgba(58, 29, 19, 0.08)`) to mimic debossed flour sack stamps and letterpress packaging.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a balanced **Rounded (`roundedness: 2`)** foundation, softening rigid rectangular web tropes into natural, bread-loaf and cookie contours:

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input text boxes, nutritional data tables, and corporate inquiry form containers.
- **Product Cards & Modal Panels (1rem / 16px):** Outer wrappers for hampers, snack jars, and workshop booking cards.
- **Signature Badges & Pills (Full rounded / 9999px):** Category filters ("Cookies", "Granola", "Pastries"), call-to-action buttons, pricing chips, and guarantee badges.
- **Seal Shapes:** Circular aspect ratios with decorative serrated or dashed border outlines to evoke physical wax seals and pantry stickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Background `#3A1D13`, label `#FBF7F0` in `Plus Jakarta Sans` semi-bold, pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `12px 28px`. Hover transforms to `#2B150E` with a slight warm shadow lift.
- **Accent CTA:** Background `#C86D46` (Terracotta) for instant purchases or limited batch pre-orders. Hover shifts to `#B45D38`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1.5px solid #3A1D13`, text `#3A1D13`. Hover fills with `#F5EFEB`.

### Badges & Dietary Chips
- Compact rounded pill tags (`padding: 4px 12px`) used for tags like "Zero Maida", "Ragi Rich", and "Sugar Free".
- Green/Botanical variant uses `#4E6E58` text over a soft sage tint (`#E8EFEA`).
- Terracotta variant uses `#C86D46` text over a warm peach tint (`#F9ECE5`).

### Product Cards
- Warm parchment background (`#FFFFFF` on `#FBF7F0` canvas), bordered with `1px solid #EBDCCF`.
- Card image container utilizes an arch or softly rounded top (`rounded-t-2xl`) showcasing high-definition, crumb-textured macro photography.
- Bottom footer contains product title in `Epilogue 700`, gram weight/quantity in muted brown, bold price, and an inline circular add-to-cart stepper.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #DCCBC0`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Focus state highlights with border `#C86D46` and a soft cinnamon focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(200, 109, 70, 0.15)`).
- Placeholder text styled in warm taupe (`#948278`).

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Custom custom-styled rounded inputs featuring `#3A1D13` check fills and warm off-white tick marks. Never pure black or system-default blue.

### Specialty Components: Hamper Builder & Artisan Seal
- **Hamper Customizer:** A modular multi-step selection tray where users mix-and-match millet snack varieties (Granola, Ragi cookies, seed crackers) with live nutritional meter updates.
- **Artisan Quality Seal:** Circular badge component featuring radial curved text reading "Millet Bakes • Ancient Grain • Modern Bite" surrounding an engraved loaf or wheat-stalk SVG emblem.
